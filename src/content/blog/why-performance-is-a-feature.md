Speed is not a vanity metric. The moment a visitor lands on your site, every
hundred milliseconds of delay is quietly shaping whether they trust you, whether
they convert, and whether search engines decide to rank you.

## Performance is conversion

Study after study shows the same pattern: faster pages convert better. When a
page responds instantly, users stay in flow. When it stalls, they bounce — often
before they have seen a single word of your message.

## Performance is SEO

Search engines fold real-world loading metrics into ranking. A fast, stable page
is easier to crawl, easier to index, and more likely to win the position you are
competing for.

## Build it in, don't bolt it on

Performance is cheapest when it is a constraint from day one: a sensible image
strategy, minimal client JavaScript, and server rendering where it counts. Retrofitting
speed onto a bloated site is painful. Designing for it is just good engineering.
